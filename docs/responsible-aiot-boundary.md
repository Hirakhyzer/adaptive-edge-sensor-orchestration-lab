# Responsible AIoT Boundary

This repository is an academic prototype for synthetic experimentation. It does not operate real devices, collect personal data, or provide deployment instructions.

## Allowed use

- Research on energy-quality trade-offs.
- Teaching examples for AIoT and edge computing.
- Synthetic benchmarking of selection policies.
- Documentation of responsible smart-city sensing assumptions.

## Not in scope

- Controlling real-world infrastructure.
- Monitoring people or private spaces.
- Safety-critical decision making.
- Deployment without institutional review.
- Claims about real-city performance without validation.

## Responsible principle

Adaptive sensing should be designed with privacy, transparency, safety, cybersecurity, accessibility, and human governance in mind.
