# Research Background

Adaptive edge sensing is concerned with deciding how a distributed sensing system can provide useful information while reducing unnecessary energy cost and communication overhead.

This repository treats sensor orchestration as a research problem rather than a deployment product. The default experiments use synthetic scenarios so that scheduling policies can be compared without relying on sensitive or operational data.

## Motivation

Always-on sensing can waste battery, increase maintenance cost, and generate redundant data. A context-aware policy can instead form a smaller group of relevant sensing nodes for each scenario.

## Research focus

- Adaptive sensor group selection.
- Energy-quality trade-off analysis.
- Context-aware scheduling at the edge.
- Reproducible evaluation using synthetic scenarios.
- Responsible AIoT boundaries for smart-city research.
