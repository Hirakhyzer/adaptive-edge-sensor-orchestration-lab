# System Design

The lab is organised around four research layers.

## 1. Scenario layer

Synthetic context variables describe demand, complexity, priority, and load. These variables are not connected to real systems.

## 2. Candidate layer

Each candidate node has a simple profile: category, zone, battery, reliability, delay, cost, and relevance.

## 3. Selection layer

The research policy ranks candidates and forms a compact group for the scenario.

## 4. Evaluation layer

The experiment reports quality score, estimated cost, and cost reduction relative to a full-use baseline.

## Design principle

The goal is to study trade-offs, not to control real-world infrastructure.
